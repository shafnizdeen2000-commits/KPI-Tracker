Summary – Labour Cost

Pick a Start date and End date — figures cover every day in between. Previous / Next move by the same number of days. Hours come from rota hours already saved (and the sample data). Grey labour cost = those hours × pay rates; type your own monthly figure to replace it. Net sales come from the Sales log (by start date). Labour % = labour cost ÷ net sales.

General

Defaults used across the system.

The VAT rate is used only when a Net figure is missing (Net = Gross ÷ (1 + VAT)). The default hourly rate is used for staff who have no rate of their own. £12.71 = UK National Living Wage (21+) from April 2026 — please check it is still current.

Automatic lock

This is a privacy lock for this computer and browser. It stops people opening the tracker without the password, but it is not bank-level encryption — keep your computer itself password-protected too.

Backup & Restore

Everything is saved in this browser on this computer — no internet needed.

Download a backup every week and keep it somewhere safe — it is the only copy if the browser data is cleared. Attached invoice files stay in the browser and are not inside the .json backup.

Erase everything

Deletes all entries, locations, staff and lists. Download a backup first.

Clear Revenue

This deletes those entries from the Sales log and Dashboard, empties the Revenue boxes and moves the dates on every page to today. You can undo it for 10 seconds afterwards.
